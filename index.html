Major project: designed and simulated an RF sensor operating near 1.5 GHz (L-band) using Ansys HFSS. The work involved full-wave electromagnetic simulation to model the sensor's resonant behavior, tune its S11/return loss, and validate frequency response before fabrication.

Embedded / Networked Control

Smart Horticulture Monitoring System

Two Arduinos, each fitted with an MCP2515 CAN controller, exchange sensor readings and control signals over a shared two-wire CAN bus instead of direct wiring or wireless. The sensor node publishes soil moisture, temperature, humidity and light on their own CAN IDs (0x100–0x103); the actuator node decodes by ID, applies threshold logic such as soil moisture below 30% switching the pump on, and acknowledges actions back on 0x200/0x201. The design is documented as a shared contract: the CAN ID table, 2-byte fixed-point payload encoding, 120 Ω termination at both bus ends, plus heartbeat frames, sensor-offline timeouts and MCP2515 bus-off recovery.

Arduino MCP2515 / SPI CAN Bus
